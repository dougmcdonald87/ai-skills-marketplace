How an entry gets in

Read, tested, numbered

Step 01

Submitted

A creator sends the form. It arrives as an email with the details filled in, and it is read by a person.

Step 02

Tested

The skill is installed and run. If it does not do what the entry says, it does not get listed, and the creator is told why.

Step 03

Published

It gets a number, a price and a publisher, and the creator reads the entry before it goes live. Numbers are never reused.

What platform-agnostic means

No lock-in

A skill is a folder of plain text files. The register lists skills that work on more than one agent, and every entry says what it actually needs. You are not buying a seat in somebody’s tool. You are buying a capability you can copy into whatever agent you already run, and move later if you want to.

How payment works

One-off, PayPal, no account

Paid

Pay once

Paid entries take a single PayPal payment through the creator’s own PayPal.me link. The register takes no commission and no cut.

Free

Go to the source

Free entries link straight to the repository, so the money stays with the person who wrote the skill and the code stays where it belongs.

Never

No subscription

No accounts, no recurring charges, no tracking and no analytics on what you look at. Prices are in GBP, one-off.

What is not here yet

Stated plainly

  • No ratings or reviews. Nobody has bought anything yet, and invented numbers are worse than no numbers.
  • No search index, ranking or personalisation. The catalogue is a list, in order.
  • No plugin, API or marketplace integration. The register is a website with a JSON file behind it.
  • No escrow, refunds or dispute handling. PayPal handles payment disputes in the ordinary way.
